1. OBJECTIVE & HYPOTHESIS
------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Evaluate whether realistic radiographic domain-variation augmentation during training on
           the BFRMX training partition improves generalization on the untouched external FracAtlas benchmark.
Hypothesis: Exposing the network to plausible variations in X-ray exposure, contrast LUTs, detector sharpness,
            and slight geometry will make feature representations more robust to scanner domain shift,
            thereby mitigating out-of-domain false-positive inflation.

3. AUGMENTATION & TRAINING CONFIGURATION
------------------------------------------------------------
Augmentation Pipeline (BFRMX Train Only):
  - RandomRotation (degrees=8)
  - RandomAffine (translate=4%, scale=0.96-1.04)
  - RandomHorizontalFlip (p=0.5)
  - ColorJitter (brightness=0.15, contrast=0.15)
  - RandomAdjustSharpness (factor=1.5, p=0.3)
  - RandomAutocontrast (p=0.2)
  - GaussianBlur (kernel=3, sigma=0.1-1.0, p=0.25)
Optimizer: AdamW (lr=1e-4, weight_decay=1e-4)
Scheduler: CosineAnnealingLR (T_max=25, eta_min=1e-6)
Loss: Unweighted CrossEntropyLoss [1.0, 1.0]
Training Time: 23.54 minutes | Best Epoch: 9
