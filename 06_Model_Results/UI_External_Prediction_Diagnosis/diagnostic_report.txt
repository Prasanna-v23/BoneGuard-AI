4. SCIENTIFIC ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS OF HIGH-CONFIDENCE EXTERNAL MISCLASSIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why does the model output high-confidence predictions on unseen external X-rays?

1. Deep CNN Feature Distribution Shift:
   Modern deep convolutional networks (such as DenseNet121) extract low-level and
   high-level textural cues. When exposed to an external dataset with differing
   radiographic exposure (kVp/mAs settings), detector pitch, and manufacturer-specific
   LUT (look-up table) tone-curves, out-of-distribution bone boundaries produce
   large activation magnitudes in the convolutional kernels, leading to extreme logit
   divergence ($z_0 - z_1 \gg 2.0$), which softmax maps to $>90\%$ confidence.

2. Severe Dataset Imbalance in FracAtlas:
   The FracAtlas external benchmark contains 3,355 normal radiographs and 713 fracture
   radiographs (82.5% normal prevalence). A false-positive rate of ~55.5% on this
   unseen distribution translates into 1,862 False Positives across the full dataset.

3. Verified In-Domain vs Out-of-Domain Contrast:
   - In-Domain BFRMX Internal Test: 99.64% Accuracy, 100.00% Precision, 0 False Positives.
   - Out-of-Domain FracAtlas Benchmark: 49.90% Accuracy, 75.32% Recall, 1,862 False Positives.
   This contrast was thoroughly researched, documented, and confirmed in Step 5 and
   Review 1. The Exp-E unweighted model is precisely the model that minimized this
   gap by reducing 447 False Positives relative to the baseline DG-CBAM model.

4. Research Integrity Adherence:
   - No retraining or threshold manipulation was performed.
   - The UI correctly reflects the exact mathematical output of the trained model.
   - The UI research disclaimers prominently inform users that BoneGuard AI is an
     investigational prototype and not an autonomous diagnostic device.
